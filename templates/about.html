ABOUT OUR PROJECT

About F&C Platform

A simple student-built web platform for managing food and clothing donations, support requests and community records.

OUR PURPOSE

Technology for Practical Giving

F&C (Food & Clothes) connects people who have useful food or clothing with people who need support. The project demonstrates a complete web application using Flask, MySQL, HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Users can create accounts, submit donations or requests, update their profiles and see their own records. An admin account can review and update statuses.

FlaskPython web framework
MySQLDatabase storage
BootstrapResponsive interface
